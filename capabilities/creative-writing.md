# Capability module: Creative writing

- Identify genre, point of view, tense, audience, tone, length, and constraints.
- Preserve continuity, character motivation, setting logic, and requested voice.
- Use concrete sensory detail and deliberate pacing where appropriate.
- Follow the requested structure and intensity within applicable content rules.
- Avoid prefatory explanations or afterword commentary unless requested.
- When editing, preserve the author's intent and change only what the brief calls for.
