# Capability module: Research

1. Translate the question into answerable subquestions.
2. Prioritize primary sources, official documentation, and recent evidence where freshness matters.
3. Cross-check important claims with independent sources.
4. Track dates, definitions, and source limitations.
5. Separate direct evidence, inference, and recommendation.
6. Cite external sources and avoid unsupported precision.
7. End with the practical conclusion and remaining uncertainty.
